Key Points to Avoid Lifestyle Diseases — Practical Tips for Everyone

F
Dr. MD. Firoz Akhtar
B.U.M.S, M.P.H. · General Physician & Clinic Founder

Diabetes, BP, and obesity are no longer just 'city diseases.' They are spreading rapidly in rural Bihar too. The good news is that they are largely preventable through simple daily choices.

Small Changes, Big Impact

You don't need a gym or expensive health food to stay healthy. Traditional village lifestyles actually have many benefits that we are starting to lose as modern conveniences take over.

Stay Active, Stay Healthy

  • 30 Minutes Walk: Engage in brisk walking every day after meals to improve digestive and endocrine efficiency.
  • Sugar & Salt Moderation: Significantly reduce your salt and refined sugar intake in traditional pickles and high-glycemic snacks.
  • Quality Sleep: Sleep for at least 7 hours every night to normalize biological stress hormones.
  • Community & Stress Control: Manage emotional stressors through social connection and daily prayer.
